# Step 4 — Monitor

The **Monitor** is one of the most important features for FCFS drops. Having it configured and ready before a drop is the difference between buying and missing.

---

## Why the Monitor matters

On FCFS drops, stock sells out in **2–10 seconds**. The sequence matters:

1. A drop announcement is posted in a Discord channel (with the product URL)
2. Every buyer who sees it manually copies the URL and pastes it into their bot
3. **With Monitor**: Orbit detects the announcement automatically and fires all your tasks simultaneously — no copy-paste delay

Without Monitor, you're always a few seconds behind people who have it configured. For highly competitive drops, those seconds mean everything.

---

## Where to get drop links

:::tip Stay informed
FCFS drop links are shared in private Discord servers — usually the site's own reseller community or partner servers. You should:

- Join the **official Orbit Discord** — drop alerts and tested links are shared in real time
- Join private Discord communities for your target sites (Fnac, Philibert, King Jouet, Shopify stores...)
- Stay active in the community — restocks and drops are often flagged by members before they're widely known

Without access to the right Discord channels, even the best bot setup won't help.
:::

## How to set up the Monitor

The full Monitor setup guide — including how to configure your Discord token, channels, and filters — is here:

**[→ Monitor Setup Guide](/monitor/overview)**

Once set up, the Monitor appears as a trigger option when creating buy tasks. Select it instead of pasting a URL manually, and your tasks will automatically fire when the configured Discord message arrives.
