# Beginner Guide

Welcome! This guide is written for people who are **completely new to botting**. You don't need any prior knowledge — just follow each step in order and you'll be generating Amazon accounts in no time.

---

## What you'll need

To generate Amazon accounts with Orbit, you need **three external services** in addition to the bot itself:

| # | What | Why | Cost |
|---|------|-----|------|
| 1 | **Residential Proxies** | To avoid IP bans from Amazon | ~5 GB per 100 accounts |
| 2 | **SMS API** | Amazon requires phone verification | $0.05–$0.30 per number |
| 3 | **CapGuru** | Solves CAPTCHA challenges automatically | ~$0.10 / 1000+ accounts |

:::tip Don't skip any of these
Without proxies, Amazon will block your IP. Without SMS, you can't complete registration. Without CapGuru, each task takes much longer (manual solving).
:::

Continue with the next pages to set up each service step by step.
