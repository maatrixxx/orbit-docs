# FCFS Sites Guide

This guide is for **FCFS (First-Come, First-Served) sites** — Fnac, Philibert, King Jouet, Shopify, and similar. These sites sell limited items that go live at a specific moment and sell out in seconds.

Unlike Amazon (which requires account generation and invite requests), FCFS sites require you to be **first in line** the moment a product drops. Orbit automates the buy flow for you — but you still need to be informed when the drop happens.

---

## What you'll need

To buy on FCFS sites with Orbit, you need **two external services** in addition to the bot:

| # | What | Why | Cost |
|---|------|-----|------|
| 1 | **SOCKS5 Proxies** | Avoid IP blocks and increase checkout speed | Variable |
| 2 | **CapSolver** | Solves CAPTCHA challenges on FCFS sites | Very cheap |

:::tip No SMS needed
FCFS sites do not require phone number verification during checkout. You don't need an SMS service for these sites.
:::

:::info Stay informed — Discord matters
The most important thing for FCFS is **knowing when the drop happens**. You should be a member of the private Discord server for each site, as well as the **Orbit Discord server**, where drops and product links are shared in real time. Without the right link at the right time, no bot can help.
:::
