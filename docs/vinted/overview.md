# Vinted

Orbit includes tools for automating actions on **Vinted**, the second-hand fashion marketplace. These tools help you monitor listings, snipe items at release, and track seller or search activity.

---

## Available Vinted Tools

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Search** | Monitors a Vinted search and sends Discord alerts for new listings |

---

## How Vinted Monitoring Works

Orbit polls your Vinted search URL at regular intervals and sends a Discord notification the moment a new listing appears that matches your criteria.

---

## Requirements

- A **Vinted account** with a verified email and payment method
- A **Discord webhook** (optional but recommended for alerts — see [Discord Settings](/settings/discord))

---

## Supported Marketplaces

Orbit supports the following Vinted regional sites:

- `vinted.fr` — France
- `vinted.be` — Belgium
- `vinted.de` — Germany
- `vinted.es` — Spain
- `vinted.co.uk` — United Kingdom
- `vinted.nl` — Netherlands
- `vinted.pl` — Poland
- `vinted.lu` — Luxembourg

Select your marketplace when configuring a Vinted task.
