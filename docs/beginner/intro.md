# What is Orbit?

## The problem

Some products are **extremely limited** — Pokémon card sets, gaming collectibles, sneakers, board games — and sell out within seconds of going live online. By the time you reload the page and click "Add to Cart", it's already gone.

Meanwhile, bots (automated programs) can react in milliseconds, add multiple items to multiple carts simultaneously, and complete checkout faster than any human ever could.

**Orbit is that bot.** It automates the entire purchase process across several sites — so you can compete on equal footing, or better.

---

## What Orbit does

Orbit handles everything that normally takes time:

- **Account creation** — generating Amazon, Fnac, or Philibert accounts in bulk
- **Invitation requests** — automatically requesting Amazon drop invitations across all your accounts
- **Add to cart & checkout** — instant ATC the moment stock goes live, then automatic checkout
- **Email & CAPTCHA handling** — OTP verification, CAPTCHA solving, all automated

You set it up once. On drop day, Orbit does the work while you do something else.
